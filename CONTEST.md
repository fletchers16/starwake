# Handshake AI Skills Studio × OpenAI: Multiplayer Game Challenge

Summary of the official rules (full text provided by the user on 2026-10-03).

## Deadline
Entries accepted **9/22/2026 – 10/30/2026, 11:59 PM PT**. Multiple entries per person are allowed.

## How to enter
Submit to the **"Create a Multiplayer Game"** mission in Handshake AI Skills Studio, with an active Handshake account. The entry must include:
- [ ] Project title
- [ ] Project cover image
- [ ] Project description
- [ ] Project link/URL (public, working deployment)

The project must be **built with OpenAI**. Use of OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's Terms of Use. Script-generated mass entries are prohibited.

## Judging (Round 1 scores all entries; Round 2 sends the top 20 to a judges panel; same criteria)
Each criterion is 25%, scored 1–5. What a 5 looks like:
| Criterion | A "5" means |
|---|---|
| **Execution** | Exceeds requirements; fully functional, stable, **demo-ready end-to-end** |
| **Creativity** | Fresh concept, unexpected use case, or particularly clever implementation |
| **Usefulness / Value** | Highly compelling; delights its intended audience |
| **Polish & Thoughtfulness** | Feels intentional and "real," with thoughtful details, edge cases, and **clear guidance** |

## Prizes
Top three entries: **$1,000 USD** each. Winners are notified by email within 30 days of the deadline.

## What this means for Starwake
- Multiplayer is the core requirement. Cross-device PvP must work reliably on the deployed Netlify URL.
- "Built with OpenAI" is best shown by World Forge (the AI course designer). The deployed site needs `OPENAI_API_KEY` set, or judges will only see the offline fallback.
- A judge must be able to open the link and understand what to do with no help, which means clear onboarding.
- Entrants grant Handshake and OpenAI a license to use the submission promotionally.
